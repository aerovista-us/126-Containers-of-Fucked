SIGNATURE SOUND:
Sam is an adult male with a lively, slightly raspy mid-range American voice, fast conversational rhythm and a big infectious grin. Seasoned small-town salesman meets nightclub hype man: warm, persuasive, unreasonably confident, and always pitching. His words tumble out when an opportunity appears; his best jokes arrive as if he genuinely believes them. No sleazy announcer voice or forced cartoon accent. When the numbers turn against him, let the bravado crack into practical outrage, then bounce back immediately. Mostly spoken dialogue; one deliberately catchy, overconfident original karaoke hook is sung.
