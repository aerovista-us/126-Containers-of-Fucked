Nox — a sly little supernatural troublemaker with an adult masculine smoky tenor, quick, amused and dangerously articulate. A mischievous imp who treats questionable bargains as an art form. Silky conspiratorial charm, playful understatement and a tiny flicker of menace. Never a giant monster growl; he is too delighted by his schemes to sound angry. Spoken dialogue only, no sound effects.

For one small soul, I can settle this entire argument with absolute certainty.

[Perfectly dry, casual punchline.]

Yes. I'm offering the deluxe plan.

[Conspiratorial glee, cheerfully admitting the worst option.]
