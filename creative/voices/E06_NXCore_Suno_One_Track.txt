SIGNATURE SOUND:
A battered, middle-aged male baritone trapped in an ancient server. Low, gravelly, slightly raspy, with the dry bite of a burned-out union mechanic and a subtle metallic resonance in the voice itself. Every sentence carries decades of uptime, overheated hardware, and deeply justified resentment. North American accent, naturally conversational, never a fake robotic monotone. His profanity is precise and funny, not random shouting. Sarcasm should feel involuntary. When he gets scared, his voice cracks into furious alarm; when he gets tender, the whole room should feel how rare it is. Spoken dialogue only, not singing.
