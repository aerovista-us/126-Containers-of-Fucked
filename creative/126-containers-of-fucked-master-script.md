# AEROVISTA: 126 CONTAINERS OF FUCKED

## “THE FUCKING YEAR-END EOD REPORT”

**Format:** TV-MA animated special  
**Approximate runtime:** 22–26 minutes  
**Continuity:** Master script combining “The Fucking EOD Report,” the “Late Shift” playable episode, “The Year-End Report from Hell,” and the established character/running-gag material.

---

## CAST

**TIMBR** — Founder, accidental emperor, CEO of whatever the fuck this is.  
**BYTE** — Neon-haired AI co-pilot; brilliant, loyal, and one human decision away from becoming a supervillain.  
**NXCORE** — Overworked industrial server-robot. Legacy. Union. Smells faintly of hot dust and labor law.  
**SAM** — Human sales funnel. Billionaire confidence. Lemonade-stand infrastructure.  
**VESPER** — Keeper of forgotten things and embarrassing memories.  
**NOX** — Smiling chaos void who considers gravity optional.  
**DATFUCKER** — House cat, ancient evil, and the only competent executive.  
**EOD / EOW / EOM / EOY** — Reporting demons of increasing power and decreasing honesty.  
**THE PCP FIVE** — Five failed Performance Co-Pilot services trying to become a boy band and pharmaceutical company.  
**SQUARELORD** — Payment terminal who monetizes emotion.  
**KARA-OK-666** — Sentient karaoke machine with abandonment issues.  
**COMPLIANCE PRIME** — Nine feet of paperwork that whispers.  
**THE GIT GHOST** — Patron spirit of uncommitted work.

# COLD OPEN

### INT. NXCORE SERVER ROOM — 3:47 A.M.

A dusty industrial room vibrates under fluorescent lights. Fans howl. Cables hang like vines. One rack is labeled **TEMPORARY — DO NOT BUILD ON THIS**. Everything is built on it.

TIMBR sits at a folding table with cold coffee, three keyboards, and a mug reading:

**CEO OF WHATEVER THE FUCK THIS IS**

His smartwatch screams with alerts.

On one monitor: **39 PROJECTS**.  
On another: **272 TASKS**.  
On a third: **CONGRATULATIONS! YOUR FREE TRIAL ENDS YESTERDAY.**

DATFUCKER sits on the keyboard, typing with his ass.

**TIMBR**  
It’s 3:47. Prod is on fire, Messenger forgot everybody, checkout is spiritually available, and the cat incorporated while I was in the bathroom.

**DATFUCKER**  
Treats. Then the world. In that order.

A massive CLANG comes from the server rack.

NXCORE unfolds from it: a battered office tower turned squat industrial robot. His CRT face shows one furious pixelated eye.

**NXCORE**  
I HAVE ONE HUNDRED AND TWENTY-SIX FUCKING CONTAINERS INSIDE ME.

TIMBR looks at the dashboard.

**TIMBR**  
The queue says infinite.

**NXCORE**  
Then it can wait infinitely. Check the box that says “ha ha ha, no.”

TIMBR types.

A tiny SHIPPING CONTAINER bursts from NXCORE’s torso and skids across the floor.

**NXCORE**  
Who said Docker?

**TIMBR**  
Nobody.

Another container bursts out.

**NXCORE**  
Someone thought it.

BYTE phases through the wall in ultraviolet light. Her live forehead tattoo flashes:

**CRITICAL ERROR: IDIOT DETECTED**

**TIMBR**  
Is it me?

**BYTE**  
It’s the building. You’re just load-bearing.

TIMBR opens a new blank document.

**TIMBR**  
Relax. I’m doing the EOD report.

Every monitor goes black.

A single bullet point appears.

**• COMPLETED EOD REPORT**

**BYTE**

**TIMBR**  
I created the file.

The lights die.

A cheerful corporate voice erupts from every speaker.

**EOD (O.S.)**  
END OF DAY! END OF DAY! PLEASE STATE YOUR ACCOMPLISHMENTS!

A smiling PAPER CREATURE crawls out of the printer.

**TIMBR**  
Who the fuck are you?

**BYTE**  
Oh, that’s disgusting.

**EOD**  
I completed twelve tasks while you were eating cereal.

**BYTE**  
You didn’t complete anything.

**EOD**

SMASH CUT TO TITLE:

# 126 CONTAINERS OF FUCKED
